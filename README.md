# 🚗URBAN WHEELS - CAR RENTAL MANAGMENT SYSTEM

A full-featured, browser-based car rental platform built with **PHP** and **MySQL**. Includes a modern customer website for browsing and booking vehicles, plus a complete admin dashboard for fleet, booking, customer, and revenue management

## 📖 OVERVIEW
Urban Wheels is a web application that lets customers:
- Browse a live fleet of 30+ vehicles
- Book a car with date selection and additional services
- Pay via **M-Pesa**, card, or cash on pickup
- Track vehicles on a real-time GPS map
- Leave reviews and contact support
- Use an AI chatbot for instant FAQ answers

Admins get a dedicated dashboard to manage everything:
- Vehicles (add, edit, delete, upload images, toggle featured)
- Bookings (approve, cancel, refund, view details)
- Users and staff
- Reviews (moderate)
- Contact messages
- About Us content (mission, vision, team, testimonials)
- GPS fleet tracking
- Revenue reports with interactive charts
## ✨ FEATURES

### CUSTOMER - FACING
- **Modern homepage** — hero section, trust bar, featured fleet, testimonials, CTA
- **Vehicle browsing** — filter by brand, model, price; view details
- **Booking system** — pickup/return dates, location, driver details, special requests
- **Additional services cart** — add GPS, chauffeur, child seat, etc.
- **Payment options** — M-Pesa STK push, card, or cash on pickup
- **My Bookings** — view, cancel, download receipts, leave reviews
- **GPS vehicle locator** — see available cars near your location (Leaflet + OpenStreetMap)
- **Interactive chatbot** — instant answers to common FAQs
- **Email notifications** — booking confirmations and receipts
- **Policy pages** — Terms, Privacy, Refund
- **Password recovery** — forgot password + reset flow

### ADMIN
- **Dashboard** — total vehicles, bookings, revenue, monthly stats, charts (Chart.js)
- **Manage Vehicles** — CRUD with image upload, featured toggle, status filter
- **Manage Bookings** — approve, cancel, refund, view full details
- **Manage Users** — activate/deactivate, view history
- **Manage Reviews** — approve or reject customer reviews
- **Manage Services** — add/edit services with icons and price types
- **Contact Messages** — reply to customer inquiries
- **About Content** — edit mission, vision, team, testimonials
- **GPS Fleet Tracking** — live map with device simulation
- **Reports** — monthly revenue, daily revenue, booking trends
- **Manage Images** — upload and assign vehicle photos
- **Auto-link images** — match filenames to vehicles automatically
